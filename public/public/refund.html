How to Request a Refund

1

Send an email to creditcleardiy@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request" within 30 days of your purchase date.

2

Please include the email address you used when purchasing and your approximate purchase date so we can locate your order.

3
Receive Your Refund

We'll process your refund within 5-10 business days. The refund will be credited back to your original payment method (credit card or debit card through Stripe).

Refund Eligibility

  • Eligible: Any purchase made within the last 30 days, for any reason

After a Refund

Once your refund is processed, your access to CreditClear DIY will be revoked. Any letter templates you've already downloaded or printed prior to the refund are yours to keep, as they are based on publicly available legal frameworks (FCRA, FDCPA). However, you will no longer have access to the letter generator, dispute tracker, or educational dashboard.

Note: We do not offer partial refunds. Refunds are for the full purchase amount ($29.99). If you purchased during a promotional period at a different price, you will receive a refund for the amount you actually paid.

Chargebacks

We ask that you contact us directly before filing a chargeback with your credit card company. We process all legitimate refund requests promptly, and contacting us first helps resolve the issue faster for everyone. Filing a chargeback without first contacting us may result in delays and additional fees.
