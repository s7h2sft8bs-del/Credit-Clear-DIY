Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 7, 2026 | Last Updated: February 7, 2026

SM Digital Solutions ("we," "us," or "our") operates CreditClear DIY. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your email address and payment information when you purchase access to CreditClear DIY. Payment processing is handled securely by Stripe, and we do not store your credit card details on our servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide and maintain our services, process your payment, send you important updates about your account, and respond to your inquiries. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data You Enter Into the Tool

CreditClear DIY allows you to generate dispute letters using personal information you enter (such as your name, address, and account details). This information is processed locally in your browser and is stored only on your device using your browser's local storage. We do not transmit or store this information on our servers.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to save your login session and any dispute tracking data you create. This data remains on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Stripe — for secure payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy can be found at stripe.com/privacy.

Render — for website hosting.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You can clear locally stored data at any time by clearing your browser's storage.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email address provided on our website.
